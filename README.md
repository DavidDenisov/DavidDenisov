# David Denisov

I’m a computer science PhD candidate at the University of Haifa, advised by Prof. Dan Feldman. My research focuses on efficient machine learning algorithms, coresets, and scalable systems. I turn theoretical guarantees into implementations that run on constrained hardware, in the browser, and at scale.

I’m open to research collaborations and to work in Israel or remotely. [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=JTG3XrwAAAAJ&hl=en)
